{%- comment -%} Assigns `term_label` for include.date: January–May Spring YYYY June–July Summer YYYY August–December Fall YYYY The academic year runs 1 August to 31 July, so Fall opens one and Spring closes it. A bare year is ambiguous about which academic year a talk sits in; the term name is not, which is why listings print this instead. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign _m = include.date | date: "%m" | plus: 0 -%} {%- assign _y = include.date | date: "%Y" -%} {%- if _m > 7 -%} {%- assign term_label = "Fall " | append: _y -%} {%- elsif _m > 5 -%} {%- assign term_label = "Summer " | append: _y -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign term_label = "Spring " | append: _y -%} {%- endif -%}
