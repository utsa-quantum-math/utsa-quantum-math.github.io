The Kochen–Specker theorem rules out hidden variables using finitely many vectors in a Hilbert space. We show that the impossibility follows from countably many axioms of real-valued infinitary logic, so it holds in every model, including countable ones that are not Hilbert spaces: hidden variables are excluded by the logic of quantum propositions, not by the continuum of Hilbert space. A game form of the model existence theorem shows that finite contradictions, like those of Kochen and Specker, still suffice.
