The Kochen–Specker theorem rules out hidden variables by exhibiting finitely many vectors in a Hilbert space to which the values 0 and 1 cannot be assigned consistently. We show that this impossibility does not need the Hilbert space itself: it follows from countably many axioms of real-valued infinitary logic and therefore holds in every model of them, including countable models that are not Hilbert spaces. Turning the model existence theorem into a game, we show that it is still witnessed by finite contradictions, with infinity entering only through countable sums.
