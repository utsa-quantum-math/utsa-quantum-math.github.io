The Kochen–Specker theorem is a statement of two-valued propositional logic: finitely many vectors in a Hilbert space admit no consistent assignment of the values 0 and 1. We lift it to the far more expressive real-valued infinitary logic, where countably many axioms forbid such assignments in every model, including countable ones that are not Hilbert spaces: hidden variables are excluded by the logic of quantum propositions, not by the continuum of Hilbert space. A game form of the model existence theorem shows that, even in this logic, finite contradictions like those of Kochen and Specker suffice.
