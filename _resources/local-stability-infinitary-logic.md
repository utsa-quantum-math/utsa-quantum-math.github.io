We develop local stability theory for the logic L<sub>ω₁ω</sub>(ℝ), the infinitary extension of Keisler's model theory of real-valued structures, in which compactness fails. The key idea is to replace all types by the types realized in elementary extensions, and for these we prove that the classical characterizations of stability hold, with canonical definitions that require no separability. The proof rests on a new double limit criterion, for arbitrary prescribed sets of limits, in Arhangel'skii's Cₚ-theory. We show that the realized types of a stable formula can form any analytic set of binary sequences containing those with finitely many 1s, so that stability does not force them to be Borel.
