In this paper we study the stability of formulas in the infinitary extension of Keisler's model theory of real-valued structures. We show that four classical characterizations of stability carry over to this logic, with the types realized in elementary extensions in place of all types: the absence of the order property, the commutation of iterated limits of the values of the formula along sequences of elements, the existence of a function on its spaces of types that extends the formula and is continuous in each variable, and the definability of types. The characterizations follow from a double limit criterion for prescribed limits, which we prove directly in the spirit of Arhangel'skii's Cₚ-theory; the Čech completeness of the spaces of Henkin types, which yields a model existence theorem in place of compactness, describes the realized types, for relational vocabularies, as analytic sets.
