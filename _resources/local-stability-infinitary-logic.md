This paper extends the theory of local stability to the logic L<sub>ω₁ω</sub>(ℝ), the infinitary extension of Keisler's model theory of real-valued structures, whose expressive power places it beyond the reach of the compactness theorem. In compact logics, the stability of a formula has four classical characterizations: the absence of the order property, the commutation of iterated limits of the values of the formula, the separately continuous extension of the formula to types, and the definability of types. We prove that all four characterizations extend to L<sub>ω₁ω</sub>(ℝ) once the types realized in elementary extensions take the place of all types. Since the realized types need not form a compact set, the proof rests on a double limit criterion for limits prescribed in advance, which we establish in the setting of Arhangel'skii's Cₚ-theory. Model existence, afforded by the Čech-completeness of the spaces of Henkin types, describes the realized types themselves and shows them to form an analytic set when the vocabulary is relational.
