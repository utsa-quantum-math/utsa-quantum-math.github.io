## Abstract

We show that adding a single probe can make the compactum of pointwise limits of a fixed family of quantum channels first countable. Forgetting the probe collapses a Cantor set of limits to one point, whereas restricting instead to preparations represented by density operators identifies pairs of limits and gives a metrizable compactum.

A channel need not act on the records of the original probe expectations. Closing the observations under the channels gives the coarsest refinement of observational equivalence that the channels respect, and every channel acts continuously on the completed records. For countably many channels and probes, on the completed records of invariant energy shards, all pointwise limits are of the first Baire class exactly when, at each fixed output coordinate, the responses of the varying computation have no independent sequence. This places the compacta of limits within Todorčević's trichotomy.

The same quantum walk has continuous limiting maps when observed at individual sites and a discontinuous limit when its position is compared with a fixed site, with metrizable compacta of limits in both cases. Measure-and-prepare channels on the binary tree realize all three nonmetrizable alternatives of the trichotomy, with exact identifications of specified indexed families.
