We give a countable theory of real-valued \\( L_{\omega_1,\omega} \\) whose standard models are the lattices of closed subspaces of separable Hilbert spaces equipped with quantum measures, and in which Gleason's theorem holds. No model of this theory, standard or not, carries a measure that takes only the values 0 and 1; thus the impossibility of hidden variables in the sense of Bell, Kochen and Specker follows from a countable theory.
