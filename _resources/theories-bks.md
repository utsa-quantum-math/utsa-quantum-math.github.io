We give a countable theory of real-valued \\( L_{\omega_1,\omega} \\) whose standard models are the lattices of closed subspaces of separable Hilbert spaces with quantum measures, so that Gleason's theorem holds in them, and no model of which carries a two-valued measure. Stating the model existence theorem as a game, we show that this impossibility is witnessed by a well-founded tree of finite obstructions, which branches infinitely only at countable suprema and infima.
