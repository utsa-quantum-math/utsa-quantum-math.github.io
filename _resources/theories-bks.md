We give a countable theory of real-valued \\( L_{\omega_1,\omega} \\) whose standard models are the lattices of closed subspaces of separable Hilbert spaces equipped with quantum measures, and in which Gleason's theorem holds; consequently, for every Borel set \\( B \\) that meets \\( [0,1] \\), no standard model has a measure whose range avoids \\( B \\). No model of this theory, standard or not, carries a measure that takes only the values 0 and 1; thus the impossibility of hidden variables in the sense of Bell, Kochen and Specker follows from a countable theory.
