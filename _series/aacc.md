A venue for faculty and students interested in the intersections of algebra,
number theory, coding theory, cryptography, and their applications. Meets
weekly on Tuesdays, 12:00&ndash;1:00&nbsp;pm, in FLN&nbsp;4.01.20 (Large
Conference Room, Mathematics Department).

The format is interactive rather than formal: alongside presentations, there
is room to discuss open problems and related research questions. No prior
background in post-quantum cryptography is required — faculty, graduate
students, and undergraduates interested in these topics are all welcome.

This semester (Fall 2026) the focus is on two major directions in
post-quantum cryptography: code-based and lattice-based cryptography, with
particular emphasis on the Linear Code Equivalence Problem, the Lattice
Isomorphism Problem, and the algebraic and structural connections between
them.
