The group's general seminar, running since 2021. Talks range across quantum
information, mathematical physics, coding theory and post-quantum
cryptography, and speakers come from within the department and from outside
it.

Meetings before 2026 are reconstructed from the department's own seminar
listing, which recorded the date, speaker and title but not the time, room or
abstract. Those fields are therefore absent rather than guessed. If you have a
better record of any of these meetings — an abstract, a flyer, slides — it
belongs in the corresponding file in `_talks/`.
