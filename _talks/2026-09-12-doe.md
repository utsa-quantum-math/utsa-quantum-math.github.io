The stabilizer formalism recasts a large class of quantum codes as fixed-point
subspaces of abelian subgroups of the Pauli group. We begin from the
Knill–Laflamme conditions \\(P E_i^\dagger E_j P = c_{ij} P\\) and show how they
specialize to a purely group-theoretic criterion for stabilizer codes.

No prior familiarity with the formalism is assumed. The second half will be a
working session on the \\([[7,1,3]]\\) Steane code.
