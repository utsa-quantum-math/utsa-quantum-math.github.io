01

The inconsistency is infinitary. Its terminal commitments are finite.

The theory T⁰¹ has no model, although every finite subset of its axioms has a model. Olivia’s winning strategy in the model existence game yields a well-founded tree of plays. At each leaf stands a finite set of commitments that no structure satisfies.

The refinement lies in this certificate and its logical dependencies. A ray configuration supplies finite geometric data; the game records how geometry, probability and countable structure force incompatible commitments.

04

The commitments retain their intervals.

Each occurrence value lies in [0, δ] or [1 − δ, 1], and each four-ray basis sum lies in [1 − η, 1 + η]. The intervals below show why sufficiently narrow commitments force exactly one high value per basis.

These are tolerances on a hypothetical dispersion-free valuation with exact rays, not a bound on experimental measurement errors.

05

The infinitary commitment selects the subspace.

In the specified infinite-dimensional strategy, countable additivity forces a finite partial sum of basis-ray probabilities close to 1. Dispersion-free commitments then single out a ray of probability close to 1. Its basis index locates the four-dimensional block in which the parity configuration is placed.

This localization belongs to the stated ray strategy. A fixed configuration of infinite-rank projections can give an infinite-dimensional obstruction using finite additivity alone.
