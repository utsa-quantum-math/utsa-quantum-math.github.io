Infinitely branching. Every branch ends.

The theory T01 axiomatizes the Hilbert-space and lattice structure together with a {0,1}-valued probability measure. It has no model, although every finite subset of its axioms has one. Olivia’s winning strategy yields a well-founded tree: Josh’s countable answers produce infinite branching, but every play ends at a finite position satisfied in no structure.

04

The parity module.

In plays that reach every commitment required by the parity argument, the terminal commitments concern nine bases of four rays inside the selected block, in the configuration of Cabello, Estebaranz and García-Alcaine. The leaf records each ray once in each of its two bases, together with an explicit identification derived from axioms that do not mention the measure. Choose a ray and omit its identification: the remaining constraints become satisfiable, so the contradiction uses every one of the eighteen.

The count tolerates error. If each value lies within δ of 0 or 1 and each basis sum lies within η of 1, it still refutes the commitments whenever 4δ + η < 1, and this holds for the ordinary ray presentation as well. What the leaf adds is where the intervals come from: they are Josh's explicit commitments, each with the challenge that forced it.

The same separation of occurrences and identifications holds for the 49-ray set found by our search: detaching any one of the 92 occurrences of a shared ray makes the remaining basis-and-identification constraints satisfiable.

The 49-ray configuration.
